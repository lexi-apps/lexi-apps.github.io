STUDIO PRINCIPLES / LEXI APPS

Built for your machine.

Every Lexi instrument follows the same engineering rules on Windows: fast startup, local data ownership, and zero visual noise.

Local-first
Your databases, translation memories, download queues, and saved keys and passwords stay on your own computer. No cloud relay stands between you and your work.
Zero ads
No banners, no tracking pixels, no telemetry beacons, and no bundled third-party adware in any installer.
No lock-in
Lexi Download Manager, LexiDictate, and Lexi Game Translator Basic run immediately after installation without creating an account.
Safe backups
Before modifying a game folder or migrating a database schema, Lexi creates an automatic local backup so you never lose work.
Self-updating
Each app checks official GitHub Releases for new versions and updates cleanly in the background.
Direct support
Built-in diagnostic issue reporting with automatic secret redaction, plus direct email contact at lexiapps@protonmail.com.

FAQ / LEXI APPS

Short answers.

Are the apps really free?

Lexi Download Manager and LexiDictate are free with no paid tiers. Lexi Game Translator Basic is free with all machine translation engines across 18 game engines; Plus ($7/month) and Pro ($12/month) are optional Patreon memberships for AI translation.

Do I need an account to use Lexi apps?

No account or sign-in is needed for Lexi Download Manager, LexiDictate, or Lexi Game Translator Basic. Only Game Translator Plus and Pro use Patreon sign-in inside the app.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. What do I do?

Windows SmartScreen may show this the first time you run an installer because the binaries are not code-signed yet (code-signing certificates are paid). Click More info, then Run anyway, and always download from our official GitHub releases. Step-by-step guide.

How can I support Lexi Apps?

You can support ongoing development and code-signing certificate costs via Buy Me a Coffee (one-time tip) or Patreon (monthly membership).

Where do I report a bug or ask a question?

Every app includes a built-in Report a problem tool under Settings / About that redacts personal paths and keys automatically. You can also write directly to lexiapps@protonmail.com.
