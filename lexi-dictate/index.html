FAQ / LEXIDICTATE

Short answers.

Is LexiDictate really free?

Yes — 100% freeware with no ads, accounts, or paid tiers. You just bring your own free Google AI Studio key.

Does selection translation burn quota automatically?

No. Highlighting text sends zero API calls until you click the icon or press Alt + Q, and the last 20 translations stay cached in RAM.

Does it interfere with games or Turkish AltGr keys?

No. Game Mode auto-pauses hotkeys and active dictation in fullscreen, borderless, and windowed games, and AltGr + Q (@) passes through cleanly.

Does LexiDictate upload my history or keys?

Never. Audio goes directly to Google AI Studio only while you hold your key; everything else stays local in %APPDATA%\LexiDictate. Read the full privacy policy.
